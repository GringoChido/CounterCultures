You asked how the scaled-down dashboard improves your team's work, and what its features, advantages, and benefits are. Here is the honest answer.

The short version
Most of the dashboard is being retired. The things that survive are the ones Odoo does not do natively, plus a small "morning glance" surface that pulls from Odoo without making your team maintain a parallel system. The biggest day-to-day value to your team is not the dashboard itself — it is the customer-facing website and the way it feeds leads into your hands. The dashboard's role is to surface things Odoo cannot, in one place, without duplication.

What survives (Features)

1. Website lead capture and the outreach view.
   Every contact form, every "request a quote," every brand-trade application on the website lands in one view with the source tagged, the team alerted by email and WhatsApp, and a clear status (new, contacted, qualified, closed). Odoo CRM begins after a lead is qualified; the dashboard owns the pre-Odoo stage where the lead is fresh off the storefront.

2. The customs / pedimento workflow (Traficos).
   Odoo has no concept of Mexican import pedimentos, broker coordination, USMCA certificates, Spanish manuals with the importer block, or the SAT cadena pre-flight. The dashboard's customs surface holds that workflow. If you prefer to manage it in Google Drive as you mentioned, the dashboard side can be retired and Drive takes over; if you want it in one place with the rest of the operations, the dashboard surface stays.

3. The customer 360 read.
   When a deal moves into Odoo, the dashboard shows a fast "everything about this customer in one place" view: order history, AR balance, last quote, last touch from the team. This is a read from Odoo, not a parallel record. It exists because clicking through Odoo to assemble the same view takes longer.

4. Morning glance (Overview).
   A single screen that shows what came in overnight: new leads, unread WhatsApp messages, invoices nearing due date, deals that have gone quiet. All of it pulled from Odoo + the website's lead capture. Your team opens it once, sees what needs attention today, then works in Odoo for the rest of the day.

5. Document inbox.
   Vendor bills, customer PO PDFs, factura attachments, broker documents — gathered from email and Drive into a single review queue so finance does not chase them across inboxes.

What stops being a dashboard surface (and goes to Odoo)
Orders, customers, invoices, payments, accounts receivable, accounts payable, P&L, pipeline, vendors, purchases, inventory, products. Your team uses Odoo for those. The dashboard's old screens for them are retired so we do not maintain two copies of the same record.

Advantages over "just use Odoo for everything"

- The lead-to-deal handoff is faster. Website leads land in a view your team can act on without logging into Odoo, with the email and WhatsApp alert already sent. Odoo gets the deal once it's qualified, not the noise of every contact form.
- Customs / pedimento has a home. If you keep the workflow in the dashboard, it sits next to the related Odoo invoice and broker emails, not in a separate spreadsheet your team has to remember to update.
- Morning glance is one click, not five. A team member opens one screen to see what needs their attention; they do not assemble it from four Odoo tabs every morning.

Advantages over "build everything custom"
We are explicitly not doing this. Anything Odoo handles natively, Odoo handles. The dashboard exists only for the things that, in your business, sit outside Odoo's model: website leads, pedimentos, the cross-system glance.

Benefits to your team

- Antonina (finance): her daily AR, AP, P&L reads stay in Odoo where she already works. The dashboard is not in her daily path; it is a place she goes when she needs to attach a broker document to a vendor bill, not where she lives.
- Javier and Ian (sales): website leads land in their queue with talking points pulled from the email history. They acknowledge them, contact, qualify, and then create the deal in Odoo. Less hunting through the storefront for who filled out which form when.
- You: the morning glance. One screen to see whether the business needs your attention today. If everything is healthy, you close it and get on with your day. If something is off, you click through to the Odoo record.

What this is not

- It is not a new system to learn alongside Odoo. The screens that duplicate Odoo are being removed.
- It is not where transactional records live. Quotes, orders, invoices, payments, bills — all in Odoo.
- It is not where your team will spend most of their day. Most of their day is in Odoo. The dashboard is the surface for the few things Odoo does not do, plus the one-screen morning view.

The fair test for whether something deserves a dashboard surface
A piece of functionality earns a place in the dashboard only if it satisfies one of two tests:

1. It is something Odoo cannot do natively (website lead capture, pedimentos).
2. It saves a measurable amount of time per day per team member by consolidating views that Odoo otherwise scatters across tabs (the morning glance, the customer 360 read).

If a screen does not pass one of those tests, it is not in the new dashboard.
