A Walkthrough

How a single inquiry
becomes a delivered order —
in ten quiet steps.

Counter Cultures sells imported plumbing and architectural fixtures — Kohler, Brizo, BLANCO, TOTO — to architects, designers and builders across Mexico. A single deal involves a public website, an inbox, a catalogue of 354,000 SKUs, a customs broker, a payment processor, and a fiscally-compliant invoice. The portal is the one place all of that lives.

In this walkthrough
  • Ten stages of one real order
  • From contact form to delivery receipt
  • Across six systems, one tab

Stage One

The buyer arrives on the storefront.

A designer specifying a new kitchen lands on countercultures.mx, finds the BLANCO QUATRUS she has been looking for, and asks for a quote.

Whether she fills the contact form, clicks Request a Quote on a product page, or simply emails — every path posts straight into the dashboard. The website is the lead funnel.

Stage Five

A quote, written live.

Roger clicks New Quote. The Document Generator opens — a smart form on the left, the rendered PDF on the right, updating as he types. Customer info auto-fills from the lead. Line items pull from a 354,000-piece catalogue via fuzzy search.

If the customer sent a spec PDF, he drops it onto the form. The system OCRs it, matches SKUs against the catalogue, and adds the line items in one click.

Stage Nine

Customs, delivery, and a stamped CFDI.

The shipment moves through twelve customs stages, each colour-coded. When it lands at the showroom, Roger generates the final Invoice — a Mexican fiscal CFDI. The system collects the customer's RFC, emails it to the accountant for stamping, and attaches the stamped XML and PDF back to the deal.

A Delivery Receipt is generated when María takes possession. All four documents live side by side in Drive.
